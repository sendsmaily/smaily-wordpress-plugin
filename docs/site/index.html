OverviewÜlevaade

Smaily Connect links your WooCommerce store to Smaily email marketing and, optionally, to Smaily Campaign Intelligence for personalised product recommendations. Smaily Connect ühendab su WooCommerce'i poe Smaily e‑turundusega ja soovi korral Smaily Campaign Intelligence'iga personaalsete tootesoovituste jaoks.

What is Smaily Connect?Mis on Smaily Connect?

Smaily Connect is a WordPress plugin that links your WooCommerce store to your own Smaily email‑marketing account and, optionally, to Smaily Campaign Intelligence — Smaily's recommendation engine for personalised products and purchase‑based automations.

It does three things:

  • Syncs contacts — customers who have opted in flow into your Smaily contact list.
  • Triggers automations — store events (welcome, first order, abandoned cart) start your Smaily workflows.
  • Powers recommendations — when you connect Campaign Intelligence, your catalog, customers and orders seed personalised recommendations and engine‑run automations.

Your data stays yours. Contacts live in your Smaily account; the plugin only connects the two systems.

Smaily Connect on WordPressi plugin, mis ühendab su WooCommerce'i poe sinu enda Smaily e‑turunduse kontoga ja soovi korral Smaily Campaign Intelligence'iga — Smaily soovitusmootoriga, mis pakub personaalseid tooteid ja ostupõhiseid automaatikaid.

See teeb kolme asja:

  • Sünkroonib kontakte — nõusoleku andnud kliendid liiguvad sinu Smaily kontaktinimekirja.
  • Käivitab automaatikaid — poe sündmused (tervitus, esimene tellimus, pooleli jäänud ostukorv) käivitavad su Smaily töövood.
  • Toidab soovitusi — kui ühendad Campaign Intelligence'i, siis su tootekataloog, kliendid ja tellimused annavad alusandmed personaalsetele soovitustele ja mootori‑automaatikatele.

Su andmed jäävad sinu omaks. Kontaktid elavad sinu Smaily kontos; plugin ainult ühendab kaks süsteemi.

How the pieces fit togetherKuidas osad kokku sobivad

There are three systems:

  • WooCommerce — your store: customers, orders, products and storefront activity.
  • Smaily — your email‑marketing account, where contacts and automation workflows live (yourcompany.sendsmaily.net).
  • Campaign Intelligence — Smaily's recommendation engine. Optional, connected with a one‑time setup link.

Smaily Connect is the bridge. WooCommerce events reach the plugin; the plugin forwards contacts and triggers to Smaily, and — if connected — catalog, customer and order data to Campaign Intelligence.

Under the hood

Background work runs on Action Scheduler (bundled with WooCommerce), not WP‑Cron. Every record carries a unique event id, so a retry never creates a duplicate. You can watch it all in the Event Log.

Süsteeme on kolm:

  • WooCommerce — sinu pood: kliendid, tellimused, tooted ja poe‑tegevus.
  • Smaily — sinu e‑turunduse konto, kus elavad kontaktid ja automaatika‑töövood (sinufirma.sendsmaily.net).
  • Campaign Intelligence — Smaily soovitusmootor. Valikuline, ühendatakse ühekordse seadistuslingiga.

Smaily Connect on sild. WooCommerce'i sündmused jõuavad pluginani; plugin edastab kontaktid ja käivitajad Smailysse ning — kui ühendatud — tootekataloogi, klientide ja tellimuste andmed Campaign Intelligence'isse.

Kapoti all

Taustatöö käib Action Scheduleri peal (tuleb WooCommerce'iga kaasa), mitte WP‑Croni peal. Iga kirje kannab unikaalset sündmuse‑id'd, seega kordus ei tekita kunagi duplikaati. Kõike saab jälgida Event Logist.

Getting startedAlustamine

Install the plugin, then work through a short 6‑step wizard. No code or command line is required. Paigalda plugin ja läbi lühike 6‑sammuline viisard. Koodi ega käsurida pole vaja.

Install the pluginPlugina paigaldus

Smaily Connect is in the WordPress plugin directory, so you install it from WordPress itself — no file to download.

  1. In WordPress admin, go to Plugins → Add New Plugin and search for Smaily Connect.
  2. Click Install Now, then Activate.
  3. A new Smaily Connect item appears in the left admin sidebar. Click it to open the Initial setup.

Later versions arrive as ordinary WordPress plugin updates, under Dashboard → Updates and on the Plugins page.

Upgrading from an older Smaily plugin?

Smaily Connect installs alongside the legacy Smaily plugin and takes over once you finish the initial setup — your existing contact sync keeps working until then. New checkouts and registrations keep syncing throughout; the once-a-day catch-up sync waits and resumes as soon as you finish the setup. Nothing is lost, and you can roll back — if you do, the older plugin asks for your Smaily API password again.

Installing from a file instead?

On a staging site, or anywhere that installs plugins from a file, the same package is attached to every release as smaily-connect.zip — download it from the Smaily Connect releases page, go to Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin, choose the ZIP and click Install Now, then Activate.

Smaily Connect on WordPressi pluginakataloogis, seega paigaldad selle WordPressist endast — faili pole vaja alla laadida.

  1. Mine WordPressi halduses Plugins → Add New Plugin ja otsi Smaily Connect.
  2. Klõpsa Install Now ja siis Activate.
  3. Vasakusse haldusribasse tekib uus Smaily Connect. Klõpsa sellel, et avada Algseadistus.

Edaspidised versioonid jõuavad kohale tavalise WordPressi plugina‑uuendusena — vaates Dashboard → Updates ja Plugins lehel.

Uuendad vanemalt Smaily pluginalt?

Smaily Connect paigaldub vana Smaily plugina kõrvale ja võtab üle alles siis, kui viisardi lõpetad — su senine kontakti‑sünk töötab kuni selle hetkeni edasi. Uued ostud ja registreerimised sünkivad kogu selle aja; kord ööpäevas käiv järelsünk ootab ära ja käivitub kohe, kui seadistuse lõpetad. Midagi ei lähe kaotsi ja saad tagasi pöörduda — kui seda teed, küsib vanem plugin sinu Smaily API parooli uuesti.

Paigaldad hoopis failist?

Staging‑saidil või kõikjal, kus pluginad paigaldatakse failist, on sama pakett lisatud igale väljalaskele nimega smaily-connect.zip — lae see alla Smaily Connecti väljalaskelehelt, mine Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin, vali ZIP ja klõpsa Install Now ning siis Activate.

Finding your way aroundMenüü

Until you finish the initial setup, clicking Smaily Connect in the admin sidebar opens the setup wizard, and clicking Settings sends you back to it — so just work through the steps in order. The wizard has 6 steps; steps after Step 1 stay locked until your Smaily connection is verified.

Once you finish the setup, Smaily Connect opens Settings, on the Connection tab. The Event Log tab shows what was sent and what failed. To go through the wizard again, use Smaily Connect → Run setup again; it starts from your saved settings.

Kuni algseadistus on lõpetamata, avab halduse külgribal Smaily Connectile klõpsamine seadistusviisardi ja Seaded (Settings) klõpsamine saadab su sinna tagasi — nii et lihtsalt läbi sammud järjekorras. Viisardil on 6 sammu; sammud pärast 1. sammu on lukus, kuni su Smaily ühendus on kinnitatud.

Kui seadistus on lõpetatud, avab Smaily Connect Seadete (Settings) lehe Ühenduse vahekaardil. Sündmuste logi vahekaart näitab, mis saadeti ja mis ebaõnnestus. Viisardi uuesti läbimiseks vali Smaily Connect → Seadista uuesti; see alustab su salvestatud seadetest.

Step 1 — Connect your Smaily accountSamm 1 — Ühenda Smaily konto

Enter your Smaily API credentials — the plugin verifies them before saving.

  • Subdomain — the part before .sendsmaily.net (e.g. yourcompany).
  • API username and API password — create these in Smaily under Settings → API.

Press Test connection. On success you'll see Verified and Connection verified. You can change these credentials later under Settings → Connection.

Upgrading from an older Smaily plugin? Your subdomain and API username are already filled in and the password field is empty — the plugin never sends a stored password back to the browser. Leave it empty and press Test connection: it uses the password already saved for that account. Type a password only if you are switching accounts or have rotated it.

Campaign Intelligence (optional) is not connected in this step. You connect it in Step 4, or later under Settings → Campaign Intelligence.

Sisesta oma Smaily API‑andmed — plugin kontrollib need enne salvestamist üle.

  • Alamdomeen — osa enne .sendsmaily.net (nt sinufirma).
  • API kasutajanimi ja API parool — need luuakse Smailys jaotises Settings → API.

Vajuta Test connection. Õnnestumisel näed Verified ja Connection verified. Neid andmeid saad hiljem muuta jaotises Settings → Connection.

Uuendad vanemalt Smaily pluginalt? Alamdomeen ja API kasutajanimi on juba täidetud ning parooli väli on tühi — plugin ei saada salvestatud parooli kunagi brauserisse tagasi. Jäta see tühjaks ja vajuta Test connection: kasutatakse juba salvestatud parooli. Sisesta parool ainult siis, kui vahetad kontot või oled parooli muutnud.

Campaign Intelligence'i (valikuline) selles sammus ei ühendata. Selle ühendad 4. sammus või hiljem jaotises Settings → Campaign Intelligence.

Step 2 — ContactsSamm 2 — Kontaktid

Choose who is synced to Smaily and which fields ride along, then bring your existing customers over.

  • Keep Sync contacts to Smaily on, and pick a consent preset (see Contacts settings). The default sends only customers who opted in.
  • Email and language are always synced; tick any additional fields you want (name, phone, and so on).
  • Optionally show a newsletter opt‑in checkbox during registration or checkout.
  • Under Initial contact import, press Start import to send your current customers to Smaily. It runs in the background — you can leave the page.

Vali, kes Smailysse sünkroonitakse ja millised väljad kaasa lähevad, seejärel too olemasolevad kliendid üle.

  • Hoia Sync contacts to Smaily sees ja vali nõusoleku‑eelseade (vt Kontaktide seaded). Vaikeväärtus saadab ainult nõusoleku andnud kliendid.
  • E‑post ja keel sünkroonitakse alati; märgi ära soovitud lisaväljad (nimi, telefon jne).
  • Soovi korral näita registreerimisel või kassas uudiskirja nõusoleku‑märkeruutu.
  • Jaotises Initial contact import vajuta Start import, et saata praegused kliendid Smailysse. See käib taustal — võid lehelt lahkuda.

Step 3 — AutomationsSamm 3 — Automaatikad

Map store events to your Smaily automation workflows. Turn on the ones you want and pick the Smaily workflow each should trigger:

  • Welcome — a shopper creates an account in your store: at checkout, or on the My Account registration form. Accounts you add in WordPress admin, or that another plugin creates, don't trigger it.
  • First order — a customer's first order.
  • Abandoned cart — a cart started but not completed. You set a delay (minutes of inactivity before the cart counts as abandoned).

Your Smaily workflows must exist in Smaily first; the plugin lists them for you to map. If none appear, none were found on your account (or Smaily couldn't be reached) — create them in Smaily, then revisit.

Seo poe sündmused oma Smaily automaatika‑töövoogudega. Lülita sisse need, mida soovid, ja vali Smaily töövoog, mida iga sündmus käivitab:

  • Welcome — ostleja loob su poes konto: kassas või „Minu konto“ registreerimisvormil. Kontod, mille lisad WordPressi haldusliideses või mille loob mõni muu plugin, seda ei käivita.
  • First order — kliendi esimene tellimus.
  • Abandoned cart — ostukorv alustatud, aga lõpetamata. Sead viivituse (tegevusetuse minutid, enne kui korv loetakse hüljatuks).

Su Smaily töövood peavad Smailys enne olemas olema; plugin loetleb need sidumiseks. Kui ühtegi ei kuva, ei leitud su kontolt ühtegi (või Smailyni ei jõutud) — loo need Smailys ja tule tagasi.

Step 4 — Campaign IntelligenceSamm 4 — Campaign Intelligence

(Optional) This is the step where you connect Campaign Intelligence. Under Connect Smaily Campaign Intelligence, paste the one‑time setup link from Smaily (https://intelligence.smaily.com/setup/…) into Setup URL and press Connect. You'll see ✓ Connected as followed by your shop's name. If you don't use Campaign Intelligence yet, press Continue → to skip this step — you can connect it later under Settings → Campaign Intelligence.

Once connected, this step seeds the engine and turns on the optional signals.

Syncing is automatic. Once connected, your products, customers and orders sync to the engine on their own — there's nothing to switch on per data type.

The catalog import starts when you connect. Connecting sends your whole product catalog to the engine once, in the background; after that only changes are sent (a product save, a stock change, a deletion). A Catalog import started notice appears right after you connect. The first products go out about 3 minutes later — press Hold back before then and nothing is sent. You can start the catalog import again any time with Import now next to Products. If a catalog import is already running, connecting again does not start a second one.

A nightly product list keeps the engine's catalog in step. Once a night, at 03:00 store time, the plugin sends the engine the list of every product the catalog sync sends — each product's code and whether it is in stock, nothing else. A product missing from the list (one deleted while a change never reached the engine) stops being recommended, and a wrong stock state is corrected. Drafts, private and pending products are not in the list. Each night's list is one catalog.manifest row in the Event Log; Details shows the list and the engine's answer. Nothing is sent that night while an import runs or product changes still wait to be sent. A store with more than 50,000 products sends no list: the row is marked failed and says why.

Import existing data: run the Customers and Orders imports (one Import now each) so the engine has history to learn from — they stay your choice and never start on their own. Products is here too, to run the catalog import again. Safe to re‑run — duplicates are impossible by design.

Browse tracking (opt‑in)

The Track browsing behavior toggle is off by default. It powers "similar products" recommendations from what shoppers view, and it is consent‑gated: browse events only fire for visitors who have granted marketing consent.

Two gates

Turning the toggle on = you allow browse tracking. The shopper's cookie‑consent banner is the second gate — both must say yes before any browse event is sent. Consent is read through the WP Consent API (see Companion plugins). Only the shopper's explicit yes counts: the consent banner must be connected to the WP Consent API and must have stored the shopper's yes for marketing. With no such banner, before the shopper answers, or after a no, nothing is sent — also where the banner treats silence as consent. The line under the toggle tells you which case your store is in — consent comes from your consent banner through the WP Consent API, or browse tracking sends nothing until a consent banner is connected to the WP Consent API, with a link to the plugin. Server‑side data (orders, customers, products) is unaffected — it always syncs.

Shopper profiling opt‑out (automatic)

Every logged‑in shopper gets a Smaily Campaign Intelligence section on their My Account dashboard with a checkbox — "Use my data for personalised recommendations." It is on by default (opt‑out model). Unticking it stops their data being used for profiling, tells the engine to stop profiling them, and is remembered on their Smaily contact too when Smaily already has one. The choice never creates a Smaily contact: for a shopper who isn't one, it is kept in the store only. Shoppers who opt out still receive emails — just non‑personalised ones.

The section appears whenever Campaign Intelligence is connected and active — whether or not you have finished the email setup wizard. (Until you finish it, an opt‑out takes effect on the store and in Campaign Intelligence, but can't be written to Smaily yet. It stays in force until the shopper opts back in, and after you finish the setup it is written to the shopper's Smaily contact, if they have one.) If you never connect it, disconnect it, or Smaily deactivates the account, shoppers don't see it — and any preference they already saved is kept for when it comes back. If a shopper's preference can't be loaded at that moment and none is known from before, the section shows a short notice asking them to try again later instead of the checkbox, together with an "Opt out of personalised recommendations" button so they can still opt out right away.

Your privacy policy must mention profiling

Because personalisation is on by default, your store's privacy policy needs a sentence saying that browsing and purchase history is used to personalise recommendations and that customers can turn it off under My Account.

Kataloogi import käivitub ühendamisel. Ühendamine saadab kogu su tootekataloogi mootorisse üks kord, taustal; pärast seda saadetakse ainult muudatused (toote salvestamine, laoseisu muutus, kustutamine). Kohe pärast ühendamist ilmub teade Kataloogi import käivitus. Esimesed tooted lähevad teele umbes 3 minuti pärast — vajuta enne seda Peata import ja midagi ei saadeta. Kataloogi impordi saad igal ajal uuesti käivitada nupuga Impordi kohe rea Tooted juures. Kui kataloogi import juba käib, ei käivita uus ühendamine teist.

Öine tooteloend hoiab mootori kataloogi ajakohasena. Kord öösel, kell 03:00 poe aja järgi, saadab plugin mootorile loendi kõigist toodetest, mida kataloogi sünkroonimine saadab — iga toote koodi ja selle, kas toode on laos, muud mitte midagi. Loendist puuduvat toodet (kustutatud toode, mille muudatus mootorini ei jõudnud) enam ei soovitata ja vale laoseis parandatakse. Mustandid, privaatsed ja ülevaatust ootavad tooted loendisse ei kuulu. Pood, kus on üle 50 000 toote, loendit ei saada: rida märgitakse ebaõnnestunuks ja see ütleb põhjuse.

Impordi olemasolevad andmed: käivita Kliendid ja Tellimused import (igaühe Impordi kohe), et mootoril oleks ajalugu, millest õppida — need jäävad sinu valikuks ega käivitu iseenesest. Siin on ka Tooted, et kataloogi importi uuesti käivitada. Kordamine on ohutu — duplikaadid on disaini poolest võimatud.

Sirvimise jälgimine (opt‑in)

Lüliti Track browsing behavior on vaikimisi väljas. See toidab "sarnaste toodete" soovitusi selle põhjal, mida külastajad vaatavad, ja on nõusolekuga piiratud: sirvimissündmused käivituvad ainult külastajatel, kes on andnud turundusnõusoleku.

Kaks väravat

Lüliti sisselülitamine = sina lubad sirvimise jälgimise. Külastaja küpsise‑nõusoleku bänner on teine värav — mõlemad peavad ütlema "jah", enne kui ükski sirvimissündmus saadetakse. Nõusolekut loetakse WP Consent API kaudu (vt Kaaslas‑pluginad). Arvesse läheb ainult ostja selgesõnaline "jah": nõusolekubänner peab olema WP Consent API‑ga ühendatud ja olema salvestanud ostja turundusele antud "jah". Sellise bännerita, enne kui ostja on vastanud, või pärast "ei"‑d ei saadeta midagi — ka seal, kus bänner loeb vaikimisi nõusolekuks. Lüliti all olev rida ütleb, kumb olukord sinu poes on — nõusolek tuleb sinu nõusolekubännerist WP Consent API kaudu või sirvimise jälgimine ei saada midagi enne, kui nõusolekubänner on WP Consent API‑ga ühendatud, koos lingiga pluginale. Serveripoolseid andmeid (tellimused, kliendid, tooted) see ei mõjuta — need sünkivad alati.

Kliendi profileerimisest loobumine (automaatne)

Igal sisselogitud kliendil on oma My Account töölaual sektsioon Smaily Campaign Intelligence märkeruuduga — "Kasuta minu andmeid personaalsete soovituste jaoks." See on vaikimisi sees (opt‑out mudel). Selle eemaldamine peatab ta andmete kasutamise profileerimiseks, käsib mootoril ta profileerimise lõpetada ja jäetakse meelde ka tema Smaily kontaktil, kui Smailys on ta juba olemas. Valik ei loo kunagi Smaily kontakti: ostleja puhul, kes pole Smaily kontakt, hoitakse seda ainult poes. Loobunud kliendid saavad ikka e‑kirju — lihtsalt mitte‑personaalseid.

Sektsioon on nähtav alati, kui Campaign Intelligence on ühendatud ja aktiivne — sõltumata sellest, kas oled e‑kirjade seadistusviisardi lõpetanud. (Kuni viisard on lõpetamata, jõustub loobumine poes ja Campaign Intelligence'is, kuid Smailysse seda veel kirjutada ei saa. Loobumine kehtib seni, kuni klient uuesti nõustub, ja pärast seadistuse lõpetamist kirjutatakse see kliendi Smaily kontakti, kui see on olemas.) Kui sa seda kunagi ei ühenda, ühenduse katkestad või Smaily konto deaktiveerib, siis kliendid sektsiooni ei näe — ja juba salvestatud eelistus jääb alles, kuni see tagasi tuleb. Kui kliendi eelistust ei õnnestu hetkel laadida ja varasemast pole seda teada, näitab sektsioon märkeruudu asemel lühikest teadet, et ta prooviks hiljem uuesti, koos nupuga "Loobu personaalsetest soovitustest", et ta saaks siiski kohe loobuda.

Su privaatsuspoliitika peab profileerimist mainima

Kuna personaliseerimine on vaikimisi sees, vajab su poe privaatsuspoliitika lauset, et sirvimis‑ ja ostuajalugu kasutatakse soovituste personaliseerimiseks ja et kliendid saavad selle My Account all välja lülitada.

Step 5 — Forms and RSS feedSamm 5 — Vormid ja RSS

Wire Smaily into your storefront. This step is mostly informational — nothing here is required to sync.

  • Newsletter signup block — add the Smaily newsletter block to any page or widget area; signups land in your Smaily contacts automatically.
  • Contact Form 7 and Elementor — if either plugin is active, a Smaily subscription field/widget is available in its editor.
  • Shortcode — the [smaily_connect_newsletter_form] shortcode gives full control over form placement in any post, page or widget. See Settings → Integrations.

AutomationsAutomaatikad

Three kinds of automation live here.

Where the workflow dropdowns get their list
  • Every dropdown reads the workflow list live from your Smaily account each time you open the settings page — there's no cache and no refresh interval. Made a change in Smaily? Reload the settings page to see it.
  • Only active workflows are listed. Deactivate a workflow in Smaily and it disappears from these dropdowns.
  • Each dropdown lists the workflows of the account its section uses — the transactional‑email sections list the separate transactional account's workflows, not your main account's.

Store‑run triggers

Map a store event to a Smaily workflow: Welcome, First order, Abandoned cart. Welcome fires when a shopper creates an account in your store (checkout or the My Account registration form) — not for accounts added in WordPress admin or created by another plugin. Abandoned cart has a delay (minutes of inactivity before a cart counts as abandoned). If no workflows appear, none were found on your Smaily account (or Smaily couldn't be reached) — create them in Smaily first. The fields each email carries for your template are listed under Merge tags.

Each trigger marks the contact — build segments on it

Every time one of these fires, it writes the moment it ran onto the contact in Smaily, so you can tell an automation‑enrolled contact from someone who subscribed themselves:

  • welcome_automation_at
  • first_order_automation_at
  • abandoned_cart_automation_at

The value is the last run's date and time in UTC (2026-08-04 14:30:00) — it's overwritten on each run, and a trigger that has never fired for a contact leaves its field untouched. Use these in Smaily to include or exclude automation‑touched contacts. The abandoned‑cart template field is_abandoned_cart is unchanged and still sent alongside.

Stop the abandoned‑cart follow‑ups once the shopper buys

When a shopper you sent an abandoned‑cart reminder to completes an order, the plugin writes one more field onto their contact:

  • abandoned_cart_purchased_at — the date and time of the purchase, in UTC, in the same format as the fields above.

Use it as the exit condition on every follow‑up step of your abandoned‑cart workflow: continue only while abandoned_cart_purchased_at is earlier than abandoned_cart_automation_at (or empty) — the shopper has bought when the purchase time is the later of the two. Both checkouts (classic and block) and both guests and logged‑in shoppers are covered, and the purchase counts as soon as the order is placed, without waiting for payment status.

It is written only for a shopper the plugin actually sent a reminder to — an ordinary purchase writes nothing and creates no contact — and only when Smaily already has that shopper as a contact: the field never creates one. When Smaily doesn't have them, nothing is sent, and the Details of the row in the Event Log show it as skipped, with the reason. The reminder's cart and product fields are left exactly as the reminder wrote them. If the shopper buys before the reminder has gone out, the reminder is dropped instead: the Event Log row shows it as cancelled.

Engine‑run recommendation automations

These are triggered by Campaign Intelligence, not by a store event — the engine enrols each contact into the Smaily workflow you pick at the moment it predicts (a replenishment running out, a customer at risk of churning). Per automation you set:

  • an Enable toggle and the Smaily workflow to use;
  • a Cooldown — minimum days between two fires for the same customer (1–365);
  • Test mode — while on, emails go only to your test addresses (comma‑separated), never to customers. Use Activate for real… to ask for real sends.
Fail‑closed by design

Test mode starts on. A test‑mode automation with no test addresses sends to no one until you add at least one address — so an automation can never fire at customers before you mean it to.

Real sends are switched on by Smaily

Activate for real… sends Smaily a request when you save. The automation stays in test mode — test addresses only — until Smaily switches real sends on after your confirmation; contact Smaily support to confirm.

After each save the plugin reads back what Campaign Intelligence stored, and the label on each automation shows it: Test mode, Waiting for Smaily's confirmation (you asked for real sends and Smaily has not switched them on yet) or Live — real sends on. The waiting label comes from your own last save on that page: after you reload the page, an automation that is still waiting shows Test mode again.

Transactional emailsTehingu‑e‑kirjad

Send your order confirmation and shipping notice through Smaily instead of WordPress's own mail, using a separate Smaily account bound only to these sends — kept apart from your marketing account so a marketing bounce or complaint can never threaten order‑email delivery.

Where to find it

Two places, on purpose: Settings → Connection tab is where you connect the account (toggle → subdomain/username/password → Test connection → the same green "✓ Connected" checkmark your main Smaily connection uses). Settings → WooCommerce tab is where the "Order confirmation" and "Shipping confirmation" sections appear — but only once that connection is established; until then the WooCommerce tab shows nothing transactional.

On the Connection tab, Use transactional emails is the master switch for the account itself; each of the two triggers on the WooCommerce tab has its own switch too, so you can turn on order confirmations without shipping notices, or vice‑versa.

Order confirmation

Sent once, the moment a customer places an order. Map it to a Smaily workflow the same way as the automations above. The fields both emails carry are listed under Merge tags.

Multilingual store: when your site has more than one language, both sections show one workflow row per language, whichever multilingual mode you picked. Each order is sent through the workflow for the language it was placed in, so you can build one single‑section workflow per language on the transactional account. An order in a language with no workflow mapped goes to the row marked Default fallback.

Shipping confirmation

Sent once, when an order first reaches a status you've marked as "Counts as shipped" — pick any number of your store's order statuses (built‑in or custom, e.g. a shipping plugin's "Shipped" status). A later flip back into a shipped status for the same order never sends a second notice.

While either toggle is on, Smaily Connect quietly takes over WooCommerce's matching built‑in customer email (Processing order / Completed order) so the customer never gets two emails for the same event. Completed‑order suppression only applies when "completed" is itself one of your chosen shipped statuses — a custom status (like "Shipped") has no built‑in WooCommerce email to replace.

Customer details

Below the two sections, Customer details → Include addresses, phone and order note adds the delivery name, the billing and delivery address, the phone number and the customer's order note to both emails. It's off by default — this is personal data, so switch it on only if your template shows it. The fields are listed under Merge tags.

If Smaily can't be reached, or briefly rejects the send, WooCommerce's own email for that order fires automatically as a fallback — a customer is never left without a confirmation. A send that keeps failing is retried for up to about an hour before this fallback kicks in, so a longer Smaily outage doesn't leave the customer waiting indefinitely. The attempt is recorded in your Event Log either way.

Two things your Smaily workflow must satisfy
  • The mapped workflow must have a single section — a multi‑section workflow sends every section at once, which isn't what a one‑shot transactional email needs.
  • A subject line that varies per order (e.g. including the order number) must be a merge tag inside the workflow template — there's no per‑send subject override from WordPress.

Saada tellimuse kinnitus ja saatmisteade Smaily kaudu (mitte WordPressi enda meili kaudu), kasutades eraldi Smaily kontot, mis on seotud ainult nende saadetistega — hoitud lahus su turunduskontost, nii et turunduse tagasipõrge või kaebus ei saa kunagi ohustada tellimuse‑e‑kirjade kohaletoimetamist.

Moving an existing order‑confirmation template

  • Map the fields. The names follow WooCommerce's own (billing_city, payment_method…); each product has its own set ending in _1…_10, and order amounts start with order_.
  • Test empty values the right way. Unused product slots are sent empty, not left out — test them with {% if product_name_2 != "" %} (or {% if product_name_2 %}). over_10_products is left out when there are 10 products or fewer, so test {% if over_10_products == "true" %}.
  • Don't add a currency sign. order_total, product_price_N and the other formatted amounts already carry it. To format money yourself, use the _raw fields.
  • If the template shows an address, the phone or the order note, switch on Customer details. Place a test order and check the email before switching WooCommerce's own email off.

For developers: two filters add your own fields. smaily_connect_transactional_email_fields ($fields, the order, and which email — order_confirmation or shipping_confirmation) adds order‑level fields; smaily_connect_transactional_email_product_fields ($fields, the order line, the order) adds per‑product fields whose names start with product_ and get the _N ending. A built‑in field can't be overwritten; names are lowercase letters, digits and _ (up to 64); values are plain text or numbers, cut at 1,000 characters; at most 20 fields per filter. Anything else is skipped (logged when WP_DEBUG is on), and a filter that fails never stops the email.

The transactional emails carry no language field. On a multilingual store, map a separate workflow per language instead (see Transactional emails).

Campaign IntelligenceCampaign Intelligence

When connected, this tab shows the connection (with Test and Disconnect) plus:

  • Import existing data — seed history so recommendations have something to learn from (Products / Customers / Orders). Safe to re‑run. The Products (catalog) import starts on its own when you connect, with a Hold back button in the notice that appears (see Step 4); Customers and Orders start only when you press Import now.
  • Browse tracking — captures product and category views to power "similar products" recommendations. Off by default; only runs when the visitor has granted marketing consent (see Step 4).
  • Recommendation attribution — credits store purchases to the recommendation that drove them. It runs automatically once the engine is connected: when a shopper lands from a recommendation link, the plugin captures the attribution parameters and stamps them onto the resulting order. No consent gate and nothing to switch on. The capture happens both on the server and in the browser (a small script the plugin loads on your storefront), so it keeps working behind a page cache and with browse tracking switched off. A click on the Recommendations block is credited to the store, and a later email click to the email — the last click wins.

Test confirms the connection is healthy. Disconnect stops recommendations and ingest; reconnecting needs a fresh setup link.

Kui ühendatud, näitab see vahekaart ühendust (koos Test ja Disconnect) ning lisaks:

  • Import existing data — anna ajalugu, millest soovitused õppida saavad (Products / Customers / Orders). Kordamine on ohutu. Toodete (kataloogi) import käivitub ühendamisel iseenesest ja ilmuvas teates on nupp Peata import (vt 4. samm); Kliendid ja Tellimused käivituvad ainult siis, kui vajutad Impordi kohe.
  • Browse tracking — jäädvustab toote‑ ja kategooria‑vaatamised, et toita "sarnaste toodete" soovitusi. Vaikimisi väljas; töötab ainult siis, kui külastaja on andnud turundusnõusoleku (vt 4. samm).
  • Recommendation attribution — omistab poe ostud soovitusele, mis need tõi. See töötab automaatselt, niipea kui mootor on ühendatud: kui klient saabub soovituslingilt, jäädvustab plugin atribuutsiooni‑parameetrid ja märgib need tekkivale tellimusele. Nõusoleku‑väravat pole ja midagi pole sisse lülitada. Jäädvustamine toimub nii serveris kui ka brauseris (plugin laadib poe lehele väikese skripti), nii et see töötab ka lehecache'i taga ja siis, kui sirvimise jälgimine on välja lülitatud. Klikk soovituste plokil omistatakse poele ja hilisem e‑kirja klikk e‑kirjale — kehtib viimane klikk.

Test kinnitab, et ühendus on terve. Disconnect peatab soovitused ja andmevoo; taasühendamiseks on vaja uut seadistuslinki.

Landing page block

The Smaily Landing Page block embeds a landing page you built in Smaily into any WordPress page or post — no code, just the page's URL. In the page or post editor, open the block inserter, search "Smaily" and add the Smaily Landing Page block (it sits in the Widgets category). Open the block's Settings panel and paste the landing page's URL (it looks like https://youraccount.sendsmaily.net/landing-pages/…) — the page appears in the editor right away; Height and Width set the size of the embed. The block needs the plugin connected to a Smaily account — it reads your account address from there — and it accepts landing pages of that account only. Anyone who may edit content can use it: the Editor role and above, not just administrators.

"Please configure the plugin first." means the plugin is not connected to a Smaily account yet (see Step 1) — or, on an older plugin version, that you are not an administrator; updating the plugin fixes that case. "Invalid landing page URL!" means the address you pasted is not a landing page of your Smaily account — copy it from Smaily again. If the landing page shows in the editor but the published page is empty, or the block comes back as "Block contains unexpected or invalid content" when you reopen the post, you are on an older plugin version: on some sites WordPress removed the embed while saving. Update the plugin, then add the block again on the affected pages.

On a page builder — Elementor, and other page builders that replace the page content — the block never reaches the visitor, because the builder replaces the page content with its own. Use the shortcode instead, in any text element: [smaily_landing_page url="https://youraccount.sendsmaily.net/landing-pages/…/html/"]. height and width are optional and take pixels or a percentage — [smaily_landing_page url="…" height="600" width="100%"]. Like the block, it accepts landing pages of your own Smaily account only; anything else shows nothing at all. On Elementor you can also use the native Smaily Landing Page widget, in the Smaily category, which takes the same three fields.

Recommendations block

The Smaily Recommendations block shows a shopper the products Smaily currently recommends to them — the same products as in their Smaily emails — as product cards with an image, name, price and link. Put it where you want it: in the page or post editor, open the block inserter, search "Smaily" and add Smaily Recommendations (it sits in the Widgets category); on a page builder, or anywhere else shortcodes work, use [smaily_recommendations]. The plugin never places it anywhere on its own.

Who sees cards: a shopper who has accepted marketing cookies and is either logged in, or a returning guest — someone who came to your store from a Smaily email before, or who bought from your store as a guest after accepting marketing cookies. Consent is read through the WP Consent API, the same marketing category and the same rule as browse tracking (see Companion plugins): only a yes the shopper gave in a consent banner connected to the WP Consent API counts. Without it nothing is shown. The page loads without waiting for the recommendations, and the cards appear a moment after it has loaded.

It shows nothing at all — no message, no empty box — to any other visitor, when Campaign Intelligence is not connected, to a logged‑in shopper who opted out of profiling (Smaily applies a guest's opt‑out itself), and when Smaily has no recommendations for the shopper yet or does not answer within 10 seconds. Prices, stock and images come from your store, and a product your store no longer shows is left out. Each shopper's answer is kept for one hour, so a new recommendation can take up to an hour to show; when Smaily cannot answer, the store asks again for that shopper after 10 minutes. A purchase that follows a click on these cards is credited to the store, not to an email. Smaily finds a logged‑in shopper by their WordPress account, so after updating the plugin run Import existing data → Customers once (see Campaign Intelligence); until then most logged‑in shoppers see nothing.

The page that carries the block is the same for every visitor, so it can stay in your page cache. The cards are fetched separately for each shopper, and that answer tells every cache never to store it (Cache-Control: no-store, private).

Connecting an Elementor Pro form to SmailyElementor Pro vormi ühendamine Smailyga

Smaily Connect can connect Elementor Pro's own form to Smaily. The form's design, its consent fields and Elementor's spam protection stay in use — there is no need to rebuild the form in Contact Form 7.

Before you start

You need:

  • an active Elementor Pro with its Forms module;
  • Smaily Connect 3.16.0 or later;
  • a connection to your Smaily account, set up and checked in Smaily Connect (see Step 1).

1. Add the Smaily action to the form

Open the page in the Elementor editor and select the Elementor Pro Form widget.

Open Actions After Submit and add the Smaily action. Keep the other actions you need, such as the email notification of a contact form.

A new Smaily section appears in the form's settings.

2. Choose the form type

The Mode setting has two options:

  • Newsletter signup — a separate form for signing up to the newsletter. Every submission that passes validation is sent to Smaily.
  • Contact form with marketing consent — a contact form with a separate marketing‑consent checkbox. The data is sent to Smaily only when that box is ticked.

For an ordinary contact form, use the second option.

3. Enter the form field IDs

Fields are matched by their ID, not by their visible label. You enter the IDs by hand.

To find an ID, open the field in the form settings and go to its Advanced tab. Copy the field's ID from there.

Smaily settingWhat to enter
Email field IDThe ID of the email field. The default is email, but check your form.
Name field IDThe ID of the name field; its value goes to the Smaily field name. Leave it empty if you don't collect a name.
Consent field IDThe ID of the marketing‑consent field. Required in contact‑form mode, and shown only in that mode.

For example, your form's IDs could be email, name and newsletter_consent. Still, use the values set in your own form.

4. Set up the consent field of a contact form

Add an Acceptance or checkbox field to the Elementor form. For example:

I want to receive [company name] newsletters.

If visitors must be able to send the contact form without signing up to the newsletter, keep the marketing‑consent field optional and unticked by default.

Enter this field's ID in the Smaily setting Consent field ID.

When the box is not ticked, nothing is sent to Smaily, but the form's other actions still run.

5. Add other data and a workflow if you want

Other fields maps more form fields to Smaily fields. On each row, set the form field's ID (Form field ID) and the name of the matching Smaily field (Smaily field).

In the Smaily field name, use lowercase letters, digits and the underscore, up to 64 characters. A row whose name has any other character — a space, a hyphen, a letter such as ä — is skipped, and so is a row that names email, is_unsubscribed or one of the three source fields listed in step 7. Only the fields you set up are sent. An empty value is not sent, so the value already in Smaily stays.

Workflow lets you pick an active Smaily workflow to start after the signup, for example a welcome email. If you don't need one, leave it on No workflow — the contact is still saved.

6. Check the spam protection

reCAPTCHA is set up in Elementor's own form, not in the Smaily section. The Smaily action runs only after Elementor's validation and spam protection have passed. If needed, follow Elementor's reCAPTCHA guide.

7. Save and test

Save the page and try the form on the public page with your own test data.

Check:

  • that the contact and the chosen fields reach Smaily;
  • that the chosen workflow starts;
  • that the contact form also works with the marketing consent unticked, without adding a new test contact to Smaily.

To test the workflow again, use another test address or wait at least five minutes: within that time, sending the same form again with the same email does not start the workflow again.

Check a successful signup and a started workflow separately.

Important: signing up again through this form marks a contact who unsubscribed from the newsletter earlier as a subscriber again.

Smaily also stores the form's name (elementor_form_name), the page address (elementor_form_url) and the time of the submission (elementor_form_submitted_at, UTC). The page address is saved only when the page is on your store's own domain. You can use these fields to see where a signup came from and to build segments (see Merge tags).

When something goes wrong

An invalid email address, or Smaily not answering, shows the visitor an error and the form can be sent again; when you are logged in as an editor, Elementor also shows you the technical reason. If the contact was saved but the workflow could not be started, the visitor sees success. The action uses the plugin's saved Smaily connection — no API credentials go into the form, and an exported form doesn't carry the workflow choice.

Importing existing dataOlemasolevate andmete import

Imports (also called backfills) seed your existing data so sync and recommendations have history to work from. Impordid (ehk backfill'id) toovad su olemasolevad andmed sisse, et sünkimisel ja soovitustel oleks ajalugu, millega töötada.

What gets importedMida imporditakse

There are four imports:

  • Contacts → your Smaily contact list (from Contacts).
  • Products, Customers, Orders → Campaign Intelligence (from Campaign Intelligence).

Each is a one‑time bulk import that runs in the background — you can leave the page and it keeps going. It is safe to re‑run: duplicates are impossible by design, because every record carries a stable id. Start the Contacts import with Start import, and the Products, Customers and Orders imports with Import now — the same button runs an import again.

The Products import is the one exception to "you start it": connecting Campaign Intelligence starts it for you, and you can hold it back from the notice before anything is sent (see Step 4).

Full history, no window

The Orders import brings in your whole order history — there is no 60‑day or any other cutoff. It counts only real sales: draft, pending, failed and trashed orders are skipped (they aren't sales), and a custom shipping status like "shipped" still counts.

Importe on neli:

  • Contacts → su Smaily kontaktinimekiri (jaotisest Kontaktid).
  • Products, Customers, Orders → Campaign Intelligence (jaotisest Campaign Intelligence).

Iga import on ühekordne masssiire, mis käib taustal — võid lehelt lahkuda ja see jätkab. Kordamine on ohutu: duplikaadid on disaini poolest võimatud, sest iga kirje kannab stabiilset id'd. Contacts impordi käivitad nupuga Alusta importi, Products, Customers ja Orders impordid nupuga Impordi kohe — sama nupp käivitab impordi ka uuesti.

Products import on erand reeglist "sina käivitad": Campaign Intelligence'i ühendamine käivitab selle sinu eest ja teatest saad selle peatada enne, kui midagi saadetakse (vt 4. samm).

Terve ajalugu, ilma aknata

Tellimuste import toob sisse su kogu tellimuste ajaloo — 60‑päeva ega muud lõikepiiri pole. See loeb ainult päris müügid: mustand‑, ootel, ebaõnnestunud ja prügikasti‑tellimused jäetakse vahele (need pole müügid), aga kohandatud tarnestaatus nagu "shipped" loeb ikka.

Companion plugins & permissionsKaaslas‑pluginad & õigused

Two optional features rely on other free plugins being present. If a feature seems to do nothing, this is usually why.

  • Browse tracking collects nothing — browse events are gated on the WP Consent API. If your store has no consent framework, wp_has_consent() is undefined and the plugin fails closed (zero events, no error). Install the free WP Consent API plugin; most consent banners register into it — CookieYes, Complianz and Real Cookie Banner all do (map their marketing/advertising category to the WP Consent API marketing category). Turn on the banner's WP Consent API setting if it has one. Only the shopper's explicit yes counts: the consent banner must be connected to the WP Consent API and must have stored the shopper's yes for marketing. With no such banner, before the shopper answers, or after a no, nothing is sent and no recommendations are shown — also where the banner treats silence as consent. Smaily Connect shows an admin notice when browse tracking is on and connected but the WP Consent API plugin is not installed.
  • Multilingual routing — per‑language contact routing reads languages from WPML, Polylang or TranslatePress. Without one of them, the site locale is used.

How consent works in each setup

Three features wait for the shopper's marketing consent: browse tracking, the Recommendations block, and the visitor token a guest buyer gets at checkout. All three use the same rule: the WP Consent API must have stored allow for the marketing category. A consent banner stores it when the shopper says yes.

  • No WP Consent API and no banner — none of the three runs. The line under the browse‑tracking toggle says so. While browse tracking is on, an admin notice says so too.
  • WP Consent API installed, but no banner records choices through it — none of the three runs, because no consent is ever stored. The admin notice cannot detect this case: the server cannot tell it apart from a banner that works only in the browser. Only the line under the browse‑tracking toggle reminds you that a yes in a connected banner is needed. This changed in this version: earlier versions tracked browsing on such a store.
  • WP Consent API and a connected banner (for example CookieYes or Complianz) — when the shopper says yes to marketing, all three run: browse tracking and the Recommendations block start on the same page, and the visitor token is created at checkout. If the shopper refuses or does not answer, none of them runs. A logged‑in shopper who turned off personalised recommendations under My Account gets no recommendations, even with consent.
  • Outside the EU — the plugin does not look at the visitor's location. It counts only a stored allow for the marketing category. If your banner stores allow by default in an opt‑out region, all three run. If the banner only treats the visitor as allowed without storing a choice, none of them runs. This is stricter than some regions require; you can set up your banner to record consent there.

Not affected by this consent:

  • Email‑link attribution — it is functional and always runs. The visitor token from a Smaily email link is stored, but the Recommendations block uses it only with consent.
  • Contact sync to Smaily — it follows your consent preset (see Contacts).
  • Automated emails — they have their own settings.
  • Orders and customers sent to Campaign Intelligence — they always sync. Smaily applies a shopper's profiling objection.
WordPress, not app permissions

Unlike an app‑store app, there are no OAuth permission prompts to approve. What a WordPress feature needs is either a capability you already have as an admin, or a companion plugin like the ones above.

Kaks valikulist funktsiooni sõltuvad teiste tasuta pluginate olemasolust. Kui mõni funktsioon näib mitte midagi tegevat, on tavaliselt põhjus siin.

  • Sirvimise jälgimine ei kogu midagi — sirvimissündmused on WP Consent API väraval. Kui su poel pole nõusoleku‑raamistikku, on wp_has_consent() defineerimata ja plugin jääb fail‑closed (null sündmust, viga ei kuva). Paigalda tasuta WP Consent API plugin; enamik nõusoleku‑bännereid registreerib sinna — CookieYes, Complianz ja Real Cookie Banner kõik teevad (seo nende turunduse/reklaami kategooria WP Consent API marketing‑kategooriaga). Kui bänneril on WP Consent API seadistus, lülita see sisse. Arvesse läheb ainult ostja selgesõnaline "jah": nõusolekubänner peab olema WP Consent API‑ga ühendatud ja olema salvestanud ostja turundusele antud "jah". Sellise bännerita, enne kui ostja on vastanud, või pärast "ei"‑d ei saadeta midagi ega näidata soovitusi — ka seal, kus bänner loeb vaikimisi nõusolekuks. Smaily Connect näitab haldusteadet, kui sirvimise jälgimine on sees ja ühendatud, aga WP Consent API pluginat pole paigaldatud.
  • Mitmekeelne suunamine — keelepõhine kontakti‑suunamine loeb keeled WPML‑ist, Polylangist või TranslatePressist. Kui ühtki neist pole, kasutatakse saidi lokaati.

Kuidas nõusolek eri seadistustes toimib

Kolm funktsiooni ootavad ostja turundusnõusolekut: sirvimise jälgimine, soovituste plokk ja külastaja‑token, mille külalisostja kassas saab. Kõigil kolmel on sama reegel: WP Consent API peab olema salvestanud kategooriale marketing väärtuse allow. Nõusolekubänner salvestab selle, kui ostja ütleb "jah".

  • WP Consent API puudub ja bännerit pole — ükski kolmest ei tööta. Seda ütleb sirvimise jälgimise lüliti all olev rida. Kui sirvimise jälgimine on sees, ütleb seda ka haldusteade.
  • WP Consent API on paigaldatud, aga ükski bänner ei salvesta valikuid selle kaudu — ükski kolmest ei tööta, sest nõusolekut ei salvestata kunagi. Haldusteade ei suuda seda olukorda tuvastada: server ei erista seda bännerist, mis töötab ainult brauseris. Ainult sirvimise jälgimise lüliti all olev rida tuletab meelde, et vaja on "jah"‑i ühendatud bänneris. See muutus selles versioonis: varasemad versioonid jälgisid sellises poes sirvimist.
  • WP Consent API ja ühendatud bänner (näiteks CookieYes või Complianz) — kui ostja ütleb turundusele "jah", töötavad kõik kolm: sirvimise jälgimine ja soovituste plokk käivituvad samal lehel ning külastaja‑token luuakse kassas. Kui ostja keeldub või ei vasta, ei tööta ükski. Sisseloginud ostja, kes on My Account all personaalsed soovitused välja lülitanud, ei saa soovitusi ka nõusolekuga.
  • Väljaspool ELi — plugin ei vaata külastaja asukohta. Arvesse läheb ainult kategooriale marketing salvestatud allow. Kui su bänner salvestab opt‑out piirkonnas vaikimisi allow, töötavad kõik kolm. Kui bänner ainult käsitleb külastajat lubatuna, aga valikut ei salvesta, ei tööta ükski. See on rangem, kui mõni piirkond nõuab; saad bänneri seadistada nõusolekut seal salvestama.

Seda nõusolekut ei vaja:

  • E‑kirja lingi atributsioon — see on funktsionaalne ja töötab alati. Smaily e‑kirja lingist tulnud külastaja‑token salvestatakse, aga soovituste plokk kasutab seda ainult nõusolekuga.
  • Kontaktide sünkroonimine Smailysse — see järgib sinu nõusoleku eelseadistust (vt Kontaktid).
  • Automaatsed e‑kirjad — neil on oma seaded.
  • Campaign Intelligence'isse saadetavad tellimused ja kliendid — need sünkivad alati. Ostja profileerimise vastuväidet rakendab Smaily.
WordPress, mitte äpi‑õigused

Erinevalt äpipoe‑rakendusest pole siin OAuth‑õiguste heakskiitmise viipasid. WordPressi funktsioon vajab kas võimekust, mis sul haldurina juba on, või kaaslas‑pluginat nagu ülal.

Failed events & retryEbaõnnestunud sündmused & kordus

The Event Log lists what the plugin sent and any failures. Each row shows a status — pending, sent or failed — and Details opens the exact request that was sent and the engine's response. A row that reads cancelled was withdrawn before it went out: an abandoned-cart reminder the shopper's purchase overtook.

  1. Open Settings → Event Log and set the Status filter to Failed (or click View only failed in the red banner).
  2. Read the Last error column. http_4xx / d6_item_error means the record was rejected (fix the record); http_5xx or a timeout is a temporary outage (a retry usually succeeds). On a Smaily send, permanent_http_401 (or 403 / 404 / 422) means Smaily refused it in a way a retry can't fix — check the account credentials or the mapped workflow first, and note that permanent_http_403 … (Smaily code 227) means the account's package does not include API access; permanent_envelope_203 means Smaily answered "invalid data" — the same data would be rejected again, so fix the contact or the email's fields before retrying; any other permanent_envelope_<code> carries Smaily's own answer for that code (for example 221: the workflow is missing, disabled or out of credit) — fix what it names before retrying; retry_limit_exceeded means it was retried five times over several hours and then given up on.
  3. Click Retry on a single row, or Retry all failed in the banner. The row flips to pending, then sent within about a minute.

A failed order or shipping confirmation offers no Retry when the customer already received WooCommerce's own email as the fallback — Details says so, and retrying would only send a second confirmation. It stays retryable in the one case where nothing reached the customer: a shipping confirmation on a shipped status of your own, which WooCommerce has no email for.

An order or shipping confirmation goes out once per order: moving an order out of a shipped status and back into it never sends a second one. When you deliberately want another — a corrected tracking number, say — find the row that was sent and click Send again. It asks you to confirm, then queues a fresh confirmation built from the order as it is now; it goes out at the next scheduled pass, within about a minute, and appears in the log as its own new row. If that second confirmation itself fails, it offers no Retry — nothing went out for it, and the confirmation the customer already has still stands. Send again is offered only on a confirmation Smaily itself sent — never on a failed row, and never on one WooCommerce's own email covered.

If the plugin turns a Retry or a Send again down — because transactional emails were switched off after the send, say — the banner tells you the reason in plain words rather than showing a technical error.

Proactive health notices

A periodic health check raises a red admin notice when something needs attention — e.g. "N sync events failed in the last 24 hours", or that the recommendation engine or the Smaily API has been unreachable for over an hour. When Smaily refuses for a reason it states outright — the account's package does not include the API, or the saved credentials are rejected — the notice says so instead, and appears at the next check rather than after an hour, because waiting cannot change that answer. Each notice links to the Event Log and clears itself once the problem is gone.

"Your Campaign Intelligence account has been deactivated"

If Smaily deactivates your Campaign Intelligence account, the plugin is told so the first time it tries to send, and a red admin notice says it plainly. Sending then stops immediately — product, customer and order syncing, imports, browsing telemetry and the engine automations all pause, so the site is not spending requests on an account that will refuse them. Nothing is lost: everything waiting in the queue stays there, and your data on the Smaily side is untouched. Only Smaily can reactivate the account — the plugin cannot bring it back on its own, so contact Smaily rather than re-running the wizard. Once the account is active again, open Settings → Campaign Intelligence, click Disconnect and connect it with a fresh setup link; syncing resumes from where it stopped. Contact sync and the Smaily email automations are a separate connection and keep running throughout.

Note: a failed row stays retryable for 90 days, so there is no rush — and a Smaily contact send that keeps failing is retried a few times with growing gaps before it lands in failed, rather than being repeated every minute forever.

Event Log loetleb, mida plugin saatis, ja iga tõrke. Iga rida näitab olekut — pending, sent või failed — ja Details avab täpse saadetud päringu ning mootori vastuse. Rida olekuga cancelled võeti enne väljasaatmist tagasi: ostukorvi meeldetuletus, millest kliendi ost ette jõudis.

  1. Ava Settings → Event Log ja sea Status‑filter Failed peale (või klõpsa punases bänneris View only failed).
  2. Loe Last error veergu. http_4xx / d6_item_error tähendab, et kirje lükati tagasi (paranda kirje); http_5xx või ajaületus on ajutine katkestus (kordus tavaliselt õnnestub). Smaily saadetise puhul tähendab permanent_http_401 (või 403 / 404 / 422), et Smaily keeldus viisil, mida kordus ei paranda — kontrolli esmalt konto andmeid või seotud automaatikat, ja pane tähele, et permanent_http_403 … (Smaily code 227) tähendab, et konto pakett ei sisalda API-ligipääsu; permanent_envelope_203 tähendab, et Smaily vastas „vigased andmed“ — samad andmed lükataks uuesti tagasi, nii et paranda enne kordust kontakti või e‑kirja välju; muu permanent_envelope_<code> kannab Smaily enda vastust sellele koodile (näiteks 221: automaatikat pole, see on välja lülitatud või krediit on otsas) — paranda enne kordust see, mida vastus nimetab; retry_limit_exceeded tähendab, et seda prooviti mitme tunni jooksul viis korda ja siis loobuti.
  3. Klõpsa üksikul real Retry või bänneris Retry all failed. Rida läheb pending peale, siis umbes minutiga sent.

Ebaõnnestunud tellimuse või saatmise kinnitusel ei ole Retry nuppu, kui klient sai varuvariandina juba WooCommerce'i enda e‑kirja — Details ütleb seda ja kordus saadaks vaid teise kinnituse. Kordus jääb alles ainult juhul, kui klient ei saanud midagi: saatmise kinnitus sinu enda saadetud‑oleku peal, mille jaoks WooCommerce'il e‑kirja ei ole.

Tellimuse või saatmise kinnitus läheb välja üks kord tellimuse kohta: tellimuse saadetud‑olekust välja ja tagasi liigutamine teist kinnitust ei saada. Kui soovid teadlikult veel ühte — näiteks parandatud jälgimisnumbriga — otsi üles rida olekus sent ja klõpsa Send again. See küsib kinnitust ja paneb järjekorda uue kinnituse, mis on koostatud tellimuse praeguse seisu põhjal; see läheb välja järgmisel plaanipärasel saatmisringil, umbes minuti jooksul, ja ilmub logisse omaette uue reana. Kui see teine kinnitus ise ebaõnnestub, ei ole sellel Retry nuppu — selle eest ei läinud midagi välja ja kliendil juba olemas olev kinnitus jääb kehtima. Send again on ainult kinnitusel, mille saatis Smaily ise — mitte kunagi ebaõnnestunud real ega sellel, mille kattis WooCommerce'i enda e‑kiri.

Kui plugin lükkab Retry või Send again tagasi — näiteks kui tehingukirjad lülitati pärast saatmist välja —, ütleb bänner põhjuse tavakeeles, mitte tehnilise veateatena.

Ennetavad terviseteated

Perioodiline tervisekontroll tõstab punase haldusteate, kui miski vajab tähelepanu — nt "N sync events failed in the last 24 hours" või et soovitusmootor või Smaily API on olnud üle tunni kättesaamatu. Kui Smaily keeldub põhjusel, mille ta ise välja ütleb — konto pakett ei sisalda API‑t või salvestatud kontoandmed lükatakse tagasi — ütleb teade seda ja ilmub juba järgmisel kontrollil, mitte tunni pärast, sest ootamine seda vastust ei muuda. Iga teade viitab Event Logile ja kaob ise, kui probleem on möödas.

"Sinu Campaign Intelligence'i konto on deaktiveeritud"

Kui Smaily deaktiveerib sinu Campaign Intelligence'i konto, saab plugin sellest teada esimesel saatmiskatsel ja punane haldusteade ütleb selle otse välja. Saatmine peatub kohe — toote-, kliendi- ja tellimuste sünkroonimine, impordid, sirvimistelemeetria ja mootori automatsioonid lähevad pausile, nii et sait ei kuluta päringuid kontole, mis need niikuinii tagasi lükkab. Midagi ei lähe kaduma: kõik järjekorras ootav jääb alles ja sinu andmed Smaily poolel jäävad puutumata. Konto saab taasaktiveerida ainult Smaily — plugin ise seda tagasi ei too, seega võta ühendust Smailyga, mitte ära käivita viisardit uuesti. Kui konto on jälle aktiivne, ava Settings → Campaign Intelligence, klõpsa Disconnect ja ühenda konto uue seadistuslingiga; sünkroonimine jätkub sealt, kus see pooleli jäi. Kontaktide sünkroonimine ja Smaily e‑kirja‑automatsioonid on eraldi ühendus ja töötavad kogu selle aja edasi.

Märkus: ebaõnnestunud rida jääb korratavaks 90 päevaks, nii et kiiret pole — ja pidevalt ebaõnnestuvat Smaily kontakti‑saadetist proovitakse mõned korrad üha pikemate vahedega, enne kui see jõuab olekusse failed, selle asemel et seda iga minut lõputult korrata.

FAQ & troubleshootingKKK & tõrkeotsing

Is it safe to re‑sync?Kas re‑sünk on ohutu?

Yes. Every import is safe to re‑run — duplicates are impossible by design. Pressing Start import or Import now again re‑runs the import from scratch, in the background, and it keeps going when you leave the page.

Jah. Iga import on ohutu korrata — duplikaadid on disaini poolest võimatud. Kui vajutad uuesti Alusta importi või Impordi kohe, jookseb import nullist läbi, taustal, ja jätkab ka siis, kui lehelt lahkud.

How often does data sync?Kui tihti andmed sünkivad?

Smaily Connect works at two speeds:

  • Near‑real‑time — when a customer or order changes, WooCommerce fires a hook and the plugin queues the change straight away. A live edit (e.g. a product price) typically reaches its destination within about a minute.
  • Every 15 minutes (background) — an Action Scheduler job retries anything still queued, processes abandoned carts, and mirrors state back where needed.

One‑time imports run when you press Start import (Contacts) or Import now (Campaign Intelligence) and then continue in the background. The catalog import also starts on its own when you connect Campaign Intelligence.

Smaily Connect töötab kahel kiirusel:

  • Peaaegu reaalajas — kui klient või tellimus muutub, käivitab WooCommerce hooki ja plugin paneb muudatuse kohe järjekorda. Reaalne muudatus (nt toote hind) jõuab sihtkohta tavaliselt umbes minutiga.
  • Iga 15 minuti järel (taust) — Action Scheduleri töö kordab kõike, mis veel järjekorras, töötleb hüljatud korve ja peegeldab seisu vajadusel tagasi.

Ühekordsed impordid käivituvad, kui vajutad Alusta importi (kontaktid) või Impordi kohe (Campaign Intelligence), ja jätkavad seejärel taustal. Kataloogi import käivitub ka iseenesest, kui ühendad Campaign Intelligence'i.

Why do I see fewer orders than expected?Miks näen oodatust vähem tellimusi?

The Orders import brings in your full history — there is no time window. If a count looks low, it's because only real sales are counted: draft, pending, failed and trashed orders are excluded (they aren't completed sales), and a store with many orders often has far fewer unique customers because of repeat buyers.

Tellimuste import toob sisse su terve ajaloo — ajaakent pole. Kui number tundub madal, on põhjus, et loetakse ainult päris müügid: mustand‑, ootel, ebaõnnestunud ja prügikasti‑tellimused on välja jäetud (need pole lõpetatud müügid), ja paljude tellimustega poel on korduvostjate tõttu sageli palju vähem unikaalseid kliente.

Is my whole customer list imported?Kas terve kliendinimekiri imporditakse?

Every registered customer account, yes. The customer import is not truncated. It covers the customer accounts on your store — shoppers who only ever checked out as a guest, without an account, are not part of the import. The guest‑order email setting under Contacts applies to new orders from then on, not to the import. A count lower than you expect is usually guest‑only buyers, or repeat buyers collapsing into fewer unique customers — not a dropped record.

Iga registreeritud kliendikonto, jah. Klientide import ei ole kärbitud. See hõlmab su poe kliendikontosid — ostlejad, kes on alati tellinud külalisena, ilma kontota, ei kuulu importi. Külalis‑tellimuste e‑posti seade jaotises Kontaktid kehtib edaspidi uutele tellimustele, mitte impordile. Oodatust madalam number on tavaliselt ainult külalisena ostnud kliendid või korduvostjad, kes koonduvad vähemateks unikaalseteks klientideks — mitte kaotatud kirje.

What does Disconnect do?Mida Disconnect teeb?

  • Disconnect Smaily — contact sync stops until you reconnect. Existing contacts and Smaily automations are untouched.
  • Disconnect Campaign Intelligence — recommendations and data ingest stop. Reconnecting needs a fresh one‑time setup link.
  • Disconnect Smaily — kontakti‑sünk peatub kuni taasühendamiseni. Olemasolevaid kontakte ja Smaily automaatikaid ei puudutata.
  • Disconnect Campaign Intelligence — soovitused ja andmevoog peatuvad. Taasühendamiseks on vaja värsket ühekordset seadistuslinki.

Does uninstalling delete my data?Kas eemaldamine kustutab andmed?

Uninstalling the plugin removes its settings and local queue data from your WordPress site. Your Smaily contacts and any Campaign Intelligence data persist — deliberately, so you can change platforms without losing your marketing history. Per‑customer erasure is always available (see below), and your stored Smaily credentials are removed on uninstall.

Plugina eemaldamine kustutab su WordPressi saidilt selle seaded ja kohaliku järjekorra andmed. Su Smaily kontaktid ja mis tahes Campaign Intelligence'i andmed jäävad alles — meelega, et saaksid platvormi vahetada ilma turundusajalugu kaotamata. Kliendipõhine kustutamine on alati saadaval (vt allpool) ja su salvestatud Smaily andmed eemaldatakse eemaldamisel.

Data & privacyAndmed & privaatsus

What data the plugin usesMis andmeid plugin kasutab

  • Customer name and email — to sync contacts to your Smaily account and, with Campaign Intelligence, as the per‑customer identity.
  • Order and product data — to power automations and, when enabled, recommendations.
  • Browse events — only when you enable browse tracking and the shopper said yes to marketing in a consent banner connected to the WP Consent API. The browser itself never sends an email or recommendation id — only an opaque visitor token. If the shopper is logged in and hasn't opted out of personalisation, the plugin attaches their email server‑side before forwarding the event, so recommendations stay linked to their account even mid‑session; the email never reaches the page or the browser.
  • Storefront recommendations — only on a page with the Recommendations block and only when the shopper said yes to marketing in a consent banner connected to the WP Consent API. Your store asks Smaily by the WordPress customer id or the visitor token — never by an email — and the browser sends nothing about the shopper.
  • Visitor‑token cookie for guest buyers — when Campaign Intelligence is connected and a shopper places an order without an account after saying yes to marketing cookies in your store's consent banner, the plugin creates a random visitor token, saves it in the smaily_rec_uid cookie (365 days by default) and on the order. It lets Smaily recognise the buyer on a later visit and show them recommendations. Only an explicit yes counts: the banner must come from a consent plugin connected to the WP Consent API and must have stored the shopper's yes (see Companion plugins). Without such a plugin, without a stored yes — also where the banner treats silence as consent — or when the shopper already has a token, nothing is created. Logged‑in buyers don't get one — they are recognised by their account.

The optional customer fields beyond email are the ones you tick in Contacts.

  • Kliendi nimi ja e‑post — kontaktide sünkimiseks su Smaily kontosse ja Campaign Intelligence'iga kliendipõhise identiteedina.
  • Tellimuste ja toodete andmed — automaatikate ja lubatuna soovituste toitmiseks.
  • Sirvimissündmused — ainult siis, kui lubad sirvimise jälgimise ja klient on WP Consent API‑ga ühendatud nõusolekubänneris turundusele "jah" öelnud. Brauser ise ei saada kunagi e‑posti ega soovituse‑id'd — ainult läbipaistmatu külastaja‑tokeni. Kui klient on sisse logitud ja ei ole personaliseerimisest loobunud, lisab plugin tema e‑posti aadressi serveripoolselt enne sündmuse edastamist, et soovitused püsiksid seotuna tema kontoga ka poole sessiooni pealt — e‑post ei jõua kunagi lehele ega brauserisse.
  • Poe soovitused — ainult soovituste plokiga lehel ja ainult siis, kui ostja on WP Consent API‑ga ühendatud nõusolekubänneris turundusele "jah" öelnud. Sinu pood küsib Smaily käest WordPressi kliendi‑id või külastaja‑tokeni järgi — mitte kunagi e‑posti järgi — ning brauser ei saada ostja kohta midagi.
  • Külastaja‑tokeni küpsis külalisostjale — kui Campaign Intelligence on ühendatud ja klient vormistab tellimuse ilma kontota pärast seda, kui on su poe küpsisebänneris turundusküpsistele "jah" öelnud, loob plugin juhusliku külastaja‑tokeni ning salvestab selle küpsisesse smaily_rec_uid (vaikimisi 365 päeva) ja tellimusele. Selle abil tunneb Smaily ostja järgmisel külastusel ära ja saab talle soovitusi näidata. Arvesse läheb ainult selgesõnaline "jah": bänner peab tulema WP Consent API‑ga ühendatud nõusolekupluginast ja olema ostja "jah"‑i salvestanud (vt Kaaslas‑pluginad). Sellise pluginata, salvestatud "jah"‑ita — ka seal, kus bänner loeb vaikimisi nõusolekuks — või kui kliendil on token juba olemas, ei looda midagi. Sisseloginud ostja tokenit ei saa — teda tuntakse ära konto järgi.

Valikulised kliendiväljad peale e‑posti on need, mille sina märgid jaotises Kontaktid.

Deletion & GDPRKustutamine & GDPR

Smaily Connect integrates with WordPress's built‑in privacy tools (Tools → Export Personal Data and Erase Personal Data):

  • Export — a data request includes the customer's data held by the plugin.
  • Erase — an erase request removes the customer from the plugin and asks Campaign Intelligence to delete them.

The erase request also empties the plugin's Smaily send queue for that address: any message still waiting to go out is deleted, so nothing is sent to someone who has asked to be forgotten. Messages that were already sent stay in the Event log as a record that they were sent, but are anonymised — the address, the name and the product details are replaced with [erased].

It also deletes the plugin's own copies of what was sent to Campaign Intelligence for that address — the customer and order records the Event log shows under Details. Those rows leave the Event log entirely. Customer and order updates for that customer that are still waiting to be sent to Campaign Intelligence are deleted too, so they cannot add the customer back. A later order or account change is a new action and is sent as usual.

Shoppers also control profiling themselves: the opt‑out under My Account → Smaily Campaign Intelligence (shown on stores with Campaign Intelligence connected) is honoured on the store and mirrored to the shopper's Smaily contact when one exists. Per‑customer erasure is available on request at any time.

Smaily Connect on lõimitud WordPressi sisseehitatud privaatsustööriistadega (Tools → Export Personal Data ja Erase Personal Data):

  • Eksport — andmepäring sisaldab plugina hoitavaid kliendiandmeid.
