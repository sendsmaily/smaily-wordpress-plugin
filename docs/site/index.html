OverviewÜlevaade

Smaily Connect links your WooCommerce store to Smaily email marketing and, optionally, to Smaily Campaign Intelligence for personalised product recommendations. Smaily Connect ühendab su WooCommerce'i poe Smaily e‑turundusega ja soovi korral Smaily Campaign Intelligence'iga personaalsete tootesoovituste jaoks.

What is Smaily Connect?Mis on Smaily Connect?

Smaily Connect is a WordPress plugin that links your WooCommerce store to your own Smaily email‑marketing account and, optionally, to Smaily Campaign Intelligence — Smaily's recommendation engine for personalised products and purchase‑based automations.

It does three things:

  • Syncs contacts — customers who have opted in flow into your Smaily contact list.
  • Triggers automations — store events (welcome, first order, abandoned cart) start your Smaily workflows.
  • Powers recommendations — when you connect Campaign Intelligence, your catalog, customers and orders seed personalised recommendations and engine‑run automations.

Your data stays yours. Contacts live in your Smaily account; the plugin only connects the two systems.

Smaily Connect on WordPressi plugin, mis ühendab su WooCommerce'i poe sinu enda Smaily e‑turunduse kontoga ja soovi korral Smaily Campaign Intelligence'iga — Smaily soovitusmootoriga, mis pakub personaalseid tooteid ja ostupõhiseid automaatikaid.

See teeb kolme asja:

  • Sünkroonib kontakte — nõusoleku andnud kliendid liiguvad sinu Smaily kontaktinimekirja.
  • Käivitab automaatikaid — poe sündmused (tervitus, esimene tellimus, pooleli jäänud ostukorv) käivitavad su Smaily töövood.
  • Toidab soovitusi — kui ühendad Campaign Intelligence'i, siis su tootekataloog, kliendid ja tellimused annavad alusandmed personaalsetele soovitustele ja mootori‑automaatikatele.

Su andmed jäävad sinu omaks. Kontaktid elavad sinu Smaily kontos; plugin ainult ühendab kaks süsteemi.

How the pieces fit togetherKuidas osad kokku sobivad

There are three systems:

  • WooCommerce — your store: customers, orders, products and storefront activity.
  • Smaily — your email‑marketing account, where contacts and automation workflows live (yourcompany.sendsmaily.net).
  • Campaign Intelligence — Smaily's recommendation engine. Optional, connected with a one‑time setup link.

Smaily Connect is the bridge. WooCommerce events reach the plugin; the plugin forwards contacts and triggers to Smaily, and — if connected — catalog, customer and order data to Campaign Intelligence.

Under the hood

Background work runs on Action Scheduler (bundled with WooCommerce), not WP‑Cron. Every record carries a unique event id, so a retry never creates a duplicate. You can watch it all in the Event Log.

Süsteeme on kolm:

  • WooCommerce — sinu pood: kliendid, tellimused, tooted ja poe‑tegevus.
  • Smaily — sinu e‑turunduse konto, kus elavad kontaktid ja automaatika‑töövood (sinufirma.sendsmaily.net).
  • Campaign Intelligence — Smaily soovitusmootor. Valikuline, ühendatakse ühekordse seadistuslingiga.

Smaily Connect on sild. WooCommerce'i sündmused jõuavad pluginani; plugin edastab kontaktid ja käivitajad Smailysse ning — kui ühendatud — tootekataloogi, klientide ja tellimuste andmed Campaign Intelligence'isse.

Kapoti all

Taustatöö käib Action Scheduleri peal (tuleb WooCommerce'iga kaasa), mitte WP‑Croni peal. Iga kirje kannab unikaalset sündmuse‑id'd, seega kordus ei tekita kunagi duplikaati. Kõike saab jälgida Event Logist.

Getting startedAlustamine

Install the plugin, then work through a short 6‑step wizard. No code or command line is required. Paigalda plugin ja läbi lühike 6‑sammuline viisard. Koodi ega käsurida pole vaja.

Install the pluginPlugina paigaldus

  1. Download the latest release ZIP from the Smaily Connect releases page.
  2. In WordPress admin, go to Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin.
  3. Choose the ZIP, click Install Now, then Activate.
  4. A new Smaily Connect item appears in the left admin sidebar. Click it to open the Initial setup.
Upgrading from an older Smaily plugin?

Smaily Connect installs alongside the legacy Smaily plugin and takes over once you finish the initial setup — your existing contact sync keeps working until then. New checkouts and registrations keep syncing throughout; the once-a-day catch-up sync waits and resumes as soon as you finish the setup. Nothing is lost, and you can roll back.

  1. Lae alla uusim väljalaske‑ZIP Smaily Connecti väljalaskelehelt.
  2. WordPressi halduses mine Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin.
  3. Vali ZIP, klõpsa Install Now ja siis Activate.
  4. Vasakusse haldusribasse tekib uus Smaily Connect. Klõpsa sellel, et avada Algseadistus.
Uuendad vanemalt Smaily pluginalt?

Smaily Connect paigaldub vana Smaily plugina kõrvale ja võtab üle alles siis, kui viisardi lõpetad — su senine kontakti‑sünk töötab kuni selle hetkeni edasi. Uued ostud ja registreerimised sünkivad kogu selle aja; kord ööpäevas käiv järelsünk ootab ära ja käivitub kohe, kui seadistuse lõpetad. Midagi ei lähe kaotsi ja saad tagasi pöörduda.

Step 2 — ContactsSamm 2 — Kontaktid

Choose who is synced to Smaily and which fields ride along, then bring your existing customers over.

  • Keep Sync contacts to Smaily on, and pick a consent preset (see Contacts settings). The default sends only customers who opted in.
  • Email and language are always synced; tick any additional fields you want (name, phone, and so on).
  • Optionally show a newsletter opt‑in checkbox during registration or checkout.
  • Under Initial contact import, press Start import to send your current customers to Smaily. It runs in the background — you can leave the page.

Vali, kes Smailysse sünkroonitakse ja millised väljad kaasa lähevad, seejärel too olemasolevad kliendid üle.

  • Hoia Sync contacts to Smaily sees ja vali nõusoleku‑eelseade (vt Kontaktide seaded). Vaikeväärtus saadab ainult nõusoleku andnud kliendid.
  • E‑post ja keel sünkroonitakse alati; märgi ära soovitud lisaväljad (nimi, telefon jne).
  • Soovi korral näita registreerimisel või kassas uudiskirja nõusoleku‑märkeruutu.
  • Jaotises Initial contact import vajuta Start import, et saata praegused kliendid Smailysse. See käib taustal — võid lehelt lahkuda.

Step 3 — AutomationsSamm 3 — Automaatikad

Map store events to your Smaily automation workflows. Turn on the ones you want and pick the Smaily workflow each should trigger:

  • Welcome — a shopper creates an account in your store: at checkout, or on the My Account registration form. Accounts you add in WordPress admin, or that another plugin creates, don't trigger it.
  • First order — a customer's first order.
  • Abandoned cart — a cart started but not completed. You set a delay (minutes of inactivity before the cart counts as abandoned).

Your Smaily workflows must exist in Smaily first; the plugin lists them for you to map. If none appear, none were found on your account (or Smaily couldn't be reached) — create them in Smaily, then revisit.

Seo poe sündmused oma Smaily automaatika‑töövoogudega. Lülita sisse need, mida soovid, ja vali Smaily töövoog, mida iga sündmus käivitab:

  • Welcome — ostleja loob su poes konto: kassas või „Minu konto“ registreerimisvormil. Kontod, mille lisad WordPressi haldusliideses või mille loob mõni muu plugin, seda ei käivita.
  • First order — kliendi esimene tellimus.
  • Abandoned cart — ostukorv alustatud, aga lõpetamata. Sead viivituse (tegevusetuse minutid, enne kui korv loetakse hüljatuks).

Su Smaily töövood peavad Smailys enne olemas olema; plugin loetleb need sidumiseks. Kui ühtegi ei kuva, ei leitud su kontolt ühtegi (või Smailyni ei jõutud) — loo need Smailys ja tule tagasi.

Step 4 — RecommendationsSamm 4 — Soovitused

Only if you connected Campaign Intelligence in Step 1. This step seeds the engine and turns on the optional signals.

Syncing is automatic. Once connected, your products, customers and orders sync to the engine on their own — there's nothing to switch on per data type.

Import existing data: run the Products, Customers and Orders backfills (one Start each) so the engine has history to learn from. Safe to re‑run — duplicates are impossible by design.

Browse tracking (opt‑in)

The Track browsing behavior toggle is off by default. It powers "similar products" recommendations from what shoppers view, and it is consent‑gated: browse events only fire for visitors who have granted marketing consent.

Two gates

Turning the toggle on = you allow browse tracking. The shopper's cookie‑consent banner is the second gate — both must say yes before any browse event is sent. Consent is read through the WP Consent API (see Companion plugins). Server‑side data (orders, customers, products) is unaffected — it always syncs.

Shopper profiling opt‑out (automatic)

Every logged‑in shopper gets a Personalised recommendations section on their My Account dashboard with a checkbox — "Use my data for personalised recommendations." It is on by default (opt‑out model). Unticking it stops their data being used for profiling, tells the engine to stop profiling them, and is remembered in Smaily too. Shoppers who opt out still receive emails — just non‑personalised ones.

Your privacy policy must mention profiling

Because personalisation is on by default, your store's privacy policy needs a sentence saying that browsing and purchase history is used to personalise recommendations and that customers can turn it off under My Account.

Ainult siis, kui ühendasid Campaign Intelligence'i 1. sammus. See samm annab mootorile alusandmed ja lülitab sisse valikulised signaalid.

Sünkroonimine on automaatne. Kui ühendatud, sünkroonivad su tooted, kliendid ja tellimused mootorisse iseenesest — andmetüübi kaupa pole midagi sisse lülitada.

Impordi olemasolevad andmed: käivita Products, Customers ja Orders backfill'id (igaühe Start), et mootoril oleks ajalugu, millest õppida. Kordamine on ohutu — duplikaadid on disaini poolest võimatud.

Sirvimise jälgimine (opt‑in)

Lüliti Track browsing behavior on vaikimisi väljas. See toidab "sarnaste toodete" soovitusi selle põhjal, mida külastajad vaatavad, ja on nõusolekuga piiratud: sirvimissündmused käivituvad ainult külastajatel, kes on andnud turundusnõusoleku.

Kaks väravat

Lüliti sisselülitamine = sina lubad sirvimise jälgimise. Külastaja küpsise‑nõusoleku bänner on teine värav — mõlemad peavad ütlema "jah", enne kui ükski sirvimissündmus saadetakse. Nõusolekut loetakse WP Consent API kaudu (vt Kaaslas‑pluginad). Serveripoolseid andmeid (tellimused, kliendid, tooted) see ei mõjuta — need sünkivad alati.

Kliendi profileerimisest loobumine (automaatne)

Igal sisselogitud kliendil on oma My Account töölaual sektsioon Personalised recommendations märkeruuduga — "Kasuta mu andmeid personaalsete soovituste jaoks." See on vaikimisi sees (opt‑out mudel). Selle eemaldamine peatab ta andmete kasutamise profileerimiseks, käsib mootoril ta profileerimise lõpetada ja jäetakse meelde ka Smailys. Loobunud kliendid saavad ikka e‑kirju — lihtsalt mitte‑personaalseid.

Su privaatsuspoliitika peab profileerimist mainima

Kuna personaliseerimine on vaikimisi sees, vajab su poe privaatsuspoliitika lauset, et sirvimis‑ ja ostuajalugu kasutatakse soovituste personaliseerimiseks ja et kliendid saavad selle My Account all välja lülitada.

Step 5 — Forms and RSS feedSamm 5 — Vormid ja RSS

Wire Smaily into your storefront. This step is mostly informational — nothing here is required to sync.

Ühenda Smaily oma poega. See samm on peamiselt informatiivne — miski siin pole sünkimiseks kohustuslik.

  • Lühikood — lühikood [smaily_connect_newsletter_form] annab täieliku paigutuskontrolli mis tahes postituses, lehel või vidinas. Vt Settings → Integrations.
  • Toodete RSS‑voog — URL, mille kleebid Smaily malliredaktorisse, et laadida poe tooted kampaaniatesse. Seadista jaotises Settings → Integrations.

ContactsKontaktid

Controls who is copied to your Smaily contact list and which fields are sent.

Sync contacts to Smaily is the master switch. When off, customers are no longer copied to Smaily; existing contacts and Smaily automations are unaffected.

Who is synced (consent presets)

Choose by your lawful basis for marketing email. The default is the consent‑safe option.

  • Subscribers only (consent) (default) — only customers who opted in to email marketing are sent.
  • All customers (legitimate interest) — every registered customer is sent regardless of marketing consent. Requires a lawful basis.
  • Checkout opt‑in only — only shoppers who accept marketing at checkout are sent (guests included).

Fields to sync

Email is always synced (it identifies the contact) and language is always synced (it routes contacts to the right account in multilingual setups). Pick any additional fields you want copied. You can also sync guest‑order email addresses.

Upgrading from an older Smaily plugin keeps the fields you had chosen there, and the tick boxes show them — so you can change them like any other setting. In the rare case your saved selection can't be read, the plugin says so in an admin notice and syncs every supported field until you tick and save a new selection.

Määrab, kes kopeeritakse su Smaily kontaktinimekirja ja millised väljad saadetakse.

Sync contacts to Smaily on pealüliti. Väljas olles kliente enam Smailysse ei kopeerita; olemasolevaid kontakte ja Smaily automaatikaid see ei mõjuta.

Keda sünkroonitakse (nõusoleku‑eelseaded)

Vali oma turunduse‑e‑kirja seadusliku aluse järgi. Vaikeväärtus on nõusoleku‑ohutu variant.

  • Subscribers only (consent) (vaikimisi) — saadetakse ainult kliendid, kes on turundusele nõusoleku andnud.
  • All customers (legitimate interest) — iga registreeritud klient saadetakse turundusnõusolekust sõltumata. Nõuab seaduslikku alust.
  • Checkout opt‑in only — saadetakse ainult kassas turunduse aktsepteerinud (sh külalised).

Sünkroonitavad väljad

E‑post sünkroonitakse alati (see identifitseerib kontakti) ja keel sünkroonitakse alati (see suunab kontaktid mitmekeelses seadistuses õigesse kontosse). Vali soovitud lisaväljad. Sünkida saab ka külalis‑tellimuste e‑posti aadresse.

Vanemalt Smaily pluginalt uuendades säilivad seal valitud väljad ja märkeruudud näitavad neid — nii saad neid muuta nagu iga teist seadet. Harval juhul, kui salvestatud valikut ei õnnestu lugeda, ütleb plugin seda haldusteates ja sünkroonib kõik toetatud väljad, kuni märgid ja salvestad uue valiku.

How consent is recordedKuidas nõusolek salvestatakse

In Subscribers only (consent) mode, a customer is synced to Smaily only when your store records them as subscribed to email marketing. Your store's own marketing‑consent record is the evidence — the plugin never assumes consent, it mirrors what your store captured.

Consent is captured by your store — the checkout marketing checkbox, the Smaily newsletter block (submitting the form is the opt‑in), or the customer's account. The record carries the marketing state, the opt‑in level, and a timestamp.

Language routing

The language sent with each contact is resolved from the customer's preferred language, then their most recent order's language, then the site's multilingual default (WPML / Polylang) — never from the request context. This keeps a scheduled sync and a live request giving the same answer, so a store with an English WordPress locale but Estonian content doesn't get its contacts mislabelled.

Subscribers only (consent) režiimis sünkroonitakse klient Smailysse ainult siis, kui su pood on ta märkinud turundusele tellijaks. Su poe enda turundusnõusoleku‑kirje on tõend — plugin ei eelda kunagi nõusolekut, vaid peegeldab seda, mille su pood salvestas.

Nõusoleku salvestab su pood — kassa turunduse‑märkeruut, Smaily uudiskirja plokk (vormi esitamine ongi nõusolek) või kliendi konto. Kirje kannab turundusseisundit, nõusoleku‑taset ja ajatemplit.

Keele suunamine

Iga kontaktiga saadetav keel tuletatakse kliendi eelistatud keelest, siis ta viimasest tellimusest, siis saidi mitmekeelsest vaikekeelest (WPML / Polylang) — mitte kunagi päringu kontekstist. Nii annavad ajastatud sünk ja reaalajas päring sama tulemuse, ja inglise WordPressi‑lokaadiga aga eesti sisuga pood ei saa oma kontakte valesti märgitud.

AutomationsAutomaatikad

Three kinds of automation live here.

Where the workflow dropdowns get their list
  • Every dropdown reads the workflow list live from your Smaily account each time you open the settings page — there's no cache and no refresh interval. Made a change in Smaily? Reload the settings page to see it.
  • Only active workflows are listed. Deactivate a workflow in Smaily and it disappears from these dropdowns.
  • Each dropdown lists the workflows of the account its section uses — the transactional‑email sections list the separate transactional account's workflows, not your main account's.

Store‑run triggers

Map a store event to a Smaily workflow: Welcome, First order, Abandoned cart. Welcome fires when a shopper creates an account in your store (checkout or the My Account registration form) — not for accounts added in WordPress admin or created by another plugin. Abandoned cart has a delay (minutes of inactivity before a cart counts as abandoned). If no workflows appear, none were found on your Smaily account (or Smaily couldn't be reached) — create them in Smaily first.

Each trigger marks the contact — build segments on it

Every time one of these fires, it writes the moment it ran onto the contact in Smaily, so you can tell an automation‑enrolled contact from someone who subscribed themselves:

  • welcome_automation_at
  • first_order_automation_at
  • abandoned_cart_automation_at

The value is the last run's date and time in UTC (2026-08-04 14:30:00) — it's overwritten on each run, and a trigger that has never fired for a contact leaves its field untouched. Use these in Smaily to include or exclude automation‑touched contacts. The abandoned‑cart template field is_abandoned_cart is unchanged and still sent alongside.

Engine‑run recommendation automations

These are triggered by Campaign Intelligence, not by a store event — the engine enrols each contact into the Smaily workflow you pick at the moment it predicts (a replenishment running out, a customer at risk of churning). Per automation you set:

  • an Enable toggle and the Smaily workflow to use;
  • a Cooldown — minimum days between two fires for the same customer (1–365);
  • Test mode — while on, emails go only to your test addresses (comma‑separated), never to customers. Use Activate for real… to go live.
Fail‑closed by design

Test mode starts on. A test‑mode automation with no test addresses sends to no one until you add at least one address — so an automation can never fire at customers before you mean it to.

Tehingu‑e‑kirjade päästikud

Tellimuse kinnitus ja Saatmisteade ilmuvad ka siia — aga alles pärast eraldi tehingu‑Smaily konto ühendamist Connection vahekaardil. Täisjuhendi leiad siit: Tehingu‑e‑kirjad.

Transactional emailsTehingu‑e‑kirjad

Send your order confirmation and shipping notice through Smaily instead of WordPress's own mail, using a separate Smaily account bound only to these sends — kept apart from your marketing account so a marketing bounce or complaint can never threaten order‑email delivery.

Where to find it

Two places, on purpose: Settings → Connection tab is where you connect the account (toggle → subdomain/username/password → Test connection → the same green "✓ Connected" checkmark your main Smaily connection uses). Settings → WooCommerce tab is where the "Order confirmation" and "Shipping confirmation" sections appear — but only once that connection is established; until then the WooCommerce tab shows nothing transactional.

On the Connection tab, Use transactional emails is the master switch for the account itself; each of the two triggers on the WooCommerce tab has its own switch too, so you can turn on order confirmations without shipping notices, or vice‑versa.

Order confirmation

Sent once, the moment a customer places an order. Map it to a Smaily workflow the same way as the automations above.

Shipping confirmation

Sent once, when an order first reaches a status you've marked as "Counts as shipped" — pick any number of your store's order statuses (built‑in or custom, e.g. a shipping plugin's "Shipped" status). A later flip back into a shipped status for the same order never sends a second notice.

While either toggle is on, Smaily Connect quietly takes over WooCommerce's matching built‑in customer email (Processing order / Completed order) so the customer never gets two emails for the same event. Completed‑order suppression only applies when "completed" is itself one of your chosen shipped statuses — a custom status (like "Shipped") has no built‑in WooCommerce email to replace.

If Smaily can't be reached, or briefly rejects the send, WooCommerce's own email for that order fires automatically as a fallback — a customer is never left without a confirmation. A send that keeps failing is retried for up to about an hour before this fallback kicks in, so a longer Smaily outage doesn't leave the customer waiting indefinitely. The attempt is recorded in your Event Log either way.

Two things your Smaily workflow must satisfy
  • The mapped workflow must have a single section — a multi‑section workflow sends every section at once, which isn't what a one‑shot transactional email needs.
  • A subject line that varies per order (e.g. including the order number) must be a merge tag inside the workflow template — there's no per‑send subject override from WordPress.

Saada tellimuse kinnitus ja saatmisteade Smaily kaudu (mitte WordPressi enda meili kaudu), kasutades eraldi Smaily kontot, mis on seotud ainult nende saadetistega — hoitud lahus su turunduskontost, nii et turunduse tagasipõrge või kaebus ei saa kunagi ohustada tellimuse‑e‑kirjade kohaletoimetamist.

Kust see leida

Kaks kohta, meelega: Settings → Connection vahekaart on koht, kus ühendad konto (lüliti → subdomeen/kasutajanimi/parool → Test connection → sama roheline "✓ Connected" märge, mida su põhi‑Smaily ühendus kasutab). Settings → WooCommerce vahekaart on koht, kus ilmuvad "Order confirmation" ja "Shipping confirmation" jaotised — aga alles siis, kui see ühendus on loodud; enne seda ei näita WooCommerce vahekaart tehingu‑e‑kirjade kohta midagi.

Connection vahekaardil on Use transactional emails pealüliti konto enda jaoks; mõlemal WooCommerce vahekaardi päästikul on ka oma lüliti, nii saad sisse lülitada tellimuskinnitused ilma saatmisteadeteta või vastupidi.

Tellimuse kinnitus

Saadetakse üks kord, hetkel kui klient tellimuse esitab. Seo see Smaily töövooga samamoodi nagu ülal olevad automaatikad.

Saatmisteade

Saadetakse üks kord, kui tellimus jõuab esimest korda staatusesse, mille oled märkinud kui "Loetakse saadetuks" — vali suvaline arv oma poe staatuseid (sisseehitatud või kohandatud, nt saatmis‑pluginate "Shipped" staatus). Hilisem tagasipöördumine samasse saadetud‑staatusesse sama tellimuse puhul teist teadet enam ei saada.

Kui kumbki lüliti on sees, võtab Smaily Connect vaikselt üle WooCommerce'i vastava sisseehitatud kliendi‑e‑kirja (Processing order / Completed order), et klient ei saaks sama sündmuse kohta kaht e‑kirja. Completed‑order e‑kirja mahasurumine kehtib ainult siis, kui "completed" ise on üks su valitud saadetud‑staatustest — kohandatud staatusel (nt "Shipped") pole asendatavat sisseehitatud WooCommerce'i e‑kirja.

Kui Smailyni ei jõuta või see lükkab saatmise korraks tagasi, käivitub varuvariandina automaatselt WooCommerce'i enda e‑kiri selle tellimuse jaoks — klient ei jää kunagi kinnituseta. Pidevalt ebaõnnestuvat saatmist proovitakse uuesti kuni umbes tund aega, enne kui see varuvariant käivitub, nii et pikem Smaily katkestus ei jäta klienti lõputult ootama. Katse jääb mõlemal juhul kirja su Event Log'i.

Kaks nõuet su Smaily töövoole
  • Seotud töövoog peab olema ühe sektsiooniga — mitme sektsiooniga töövoog saadab kõik sektsioonid korraga, mis ei sobi ühekordse tehingu‑e‑kirja jaoks.
  • Tellimuse kohta muutuv teemarida (nt tellimuse numbriga) peab olema töövoo malli sees liitmärgend (merge tag) — WordPressist teemarea ülekirjutamist saadetise kohta pole.

Campaign IntelligenceCampaign Intelligence

When connected, this tab shows the connection (with Test and Disconnect) plus:

  • Import existing data — seed history so recommendations have something to learn from (Products / Customers / Orders). Safe to re‑run.
  • Browse tracking — captures product and category views to power "similar products" recommendations. Off by default; only runs when the visitor has granted marketing consent (see Step 4).
  • Recommendation attribution — credits store purchases to the recommendation that drove them. It runs automatically once the engine is connected: when a shopper lands from a recommendation link, the plugin captures the attribution parameters and stamps them onto the resulting order. No consent gate and nothing to switch on. The capture happens both on the server and in the browser (a small script the plugin loads on your storefront), so it keeps working behind a page cache and with browse tracking switched off.

Test confirms the connection is healthy. Disconnect stops recommendations and ingest; reconnecting needs a fresh setup link.

Kui ühendatud, näitab see vahekaart ühendust (koos Test ja Disconnect) ning lisaks:

  • Import existing data — anna ajalugu, millest soovitused õppida saavad (Products / Customers / Orders). Kordamine on ohutu.
  • Browse tracking — jäädvustab toote‑ ja kategooria‑vaatamised, et toita "sarnaste toodete" soovitusi. Vaikimisi väljas; töötab ainult siis, kui külastaja on andnud turundusnõusoleku (vt 4. samm).
  • Recommendation attribution — omistab poe ostud soovitusele, mis need tõi. See töötab automaatselt, niipea kui mootor on ühendatud: kui klient saabub soovituslingilt, jäädvustab plugin atribuutsiooni‑parameetrid ja märgib need tekkivale tellimusele. Nõusoleku‑väravat pole ja midagi pole sisse lülitada. Jäädvustamine toimub nii serveris kui ka brauseris (plugin laadib poe lehele väikese skripti), nii et see töötab ka lehecache'i taga ja siis, kui sirvimise jälgimine on välja lülitatud.

Importing existing dataOlemasolevate andmete import

Imports (also called backfills) seed your existing data so sync and recommendations have history to work from. Impordid (ehk backfill'id) toovad su olemasolevad andmed sisse, et sünkimisel ja soovitustel oleks ajalugu, millega töötada.

What gets importedMida imporditakse

There are four imports:

  • Contacts → your Smaily contact list (from Contacts).
  • Products, Customers, Orders → Campaign Intelligence (from Campaign Intelligence).

Each is a one‑time bulk import that runs in the background — you can leave the page and it keeps going. It is safe to re‑run: duplicates are impossible by design, because every record carries a stable id. Press Start the first time, Sync again afterwards.

Full history, no window

The Orders import brings in your whole order history — there is no 60‑day or any other cutoff. It counts only real sales: draft, pending, failed and trashed orders are skipped (they aren't sales), and a custom shipping status like "shipped" still counts.

Importe on neli:

  • Contacts → su Smaily kontaktinimekiri (jaotisest Kontaktid).
  • Products, Customers, Orders → Campaign Intelligence (jaotisest Campaign Intelligence).

Iga import on ühekordne masssiire, mis käib taustal — võid lehelt lahkuda ja see jätkab. Kordamine on ohutu: duplikaadid on disaini poolest võimatud, sest iga kirje kannab stabiilset id'd. Vajuta esimest korda Start, seejärel Sync again.

Terve ajalugu, ilma aknata

Tellimuste import toob sisse su kogu tellimuste ajaloo — 60‑päeva ega muud lõikepiiri pole. See loeb ainult päris müügid: mustand‑, ootel, ebaõnnestunud ja prügikasti‑tellimused jäetakse vahele (need pole müügid), aga kohandatud tarnestaatus nagu "shipped" loeb ikka.

Companion plugins & permissionsKaaslas‑pluginad & õigused

Two optional features rely on other free plugins being present. If a feature seems to do nothing, this is usually why.

  • Browse tracking collects nothing — browse events are gated on the WP Consent API. If your store has no consent framework, wp_has_consent() is undefined and the plugin fails closed (zero events, no error). Install the free WP Consent API plugin; most consent banners register into it — CookieYes, Complianz and Real Cookie Banner all do (map their marketing/advertising category to the WP Consent API marketing category). Smaily Connect shows an admin notice when browse tracking is on and connected but no consent signal is available.
  • Multilingual routing — per‑language contact routing reads languages from WPML or Polylang. Without either, the site locale is used.
WordPress, not app permissions

Unlike an app‑store app, there are no OAuth permission prompts to approve. What a WordPress feature needs is either a capability you already have as an admin, or a companion plugin like the ones above.

Kaks valikulist funktsiooni sõltuvad teiste tasuta pluginate olemasolust. Kui mõni funktsioon näib mitte midagi tegevat, on tavaliselt põhjus siin.

  • Sirvimise jälgimine ei kogu midagi — sirvimissündmused on WP Consent API väraval. Kui su poel pole nõusoleku‑raamistikku, on wp_has_consent() defineerimata ja plugin jääb fail‑closed (null sündmust, viga ei kuva). Failed events & retryEbaõnnestunud sündmused & kordus

The Event Log lists what the plugin sent and any failures. Each row shows a status — pending, sent or failed — and Details opens the exact request that was sent and the engine's response.

  1. Open Settings → Event Log and set the Status filter to Failed (or click View only failed in the red banner).
  2. Read the Last error column. http_4xx / d6_item_error means the record was rejected (fix the record); http_5xx or a timeout is a temporary outage (a retry usually succeeds). On a Smaily send, permanent_http_401 (or 403 / 404 / 422) means Smaily refused it in a way a retry can't fix — check the account credentials or the mapped workflow first, and note that permanent_http_403 … (Smaily code 227) means the account's package does not include API access; retry_limit_exceeded means it was retried five times over several hours and then given up on.
  3. Click Retry on a single row, or Retry all failed in the banner. The row flips to pending, then sent within about a minute.
Proactive health notices

A periodic health check raises a red admin notice when something needs attention — e.g. "N sync events failed in the last 24 hours", or that the recommendation engine or the Smaily API has been unreachable for over an hour. When Smaily refuses for a reason it states outright — the account's package does not include the API, or the saved credentials are rejected — the notice says so instead, and appears at the next check rather than after an hour, because waiting cannot change that answer. Each notice links to the Event Log and clears itself once the problem is gone.

Note: a failed row stays retryable for 90 days, so there is no rush — and a Smaily contact send that keeps failing is retried a few times with growing gaps before it lands in failed, rather than being repeated every minute forever.

Event Log loetleb, mida plugin saatis, ja iga tõrke. Iga rida näitab olekut — pending, sent või failed — ja Details avab täpse saadetud päringu ning mootori vastuse.

  1. Ava Settings → Event Log ja sea Status‑filter Failed peale (või klõpsa punases bänneris View only failed).

  3. Klõpsa üksikul real Retry või bänneris Retry all failed. Rida läheb pending peale, siis umbes minutiga sent.
Ennetavad terviseteated

FAQ & troubleshootingKKK & tõrkeotsing

Is it safe to re‑sync?Kas re‑sünk on ohutu?

Yes. Every import is safe to re‑run — duplicates are impossible by design. Sync again re‑runs from scratch, in the background, and survives you leaving the page.

Jah. Iga import on ohutu korrata — duplikaadid on disaini poolest võimatud. Sync again jooksutab nullist, taustal, ja jääb püsima ka siis, kui lehelt lahkud.

How often does data sync?Kui tihti andmed sünkivad?

Smaily Connect works at two speeds:

  • Near‑real‑time — when a customer or order changes, WooCommerce fires a hook and the plugin queues the change straight away. A live edit (e.g. a product price) typically reaches its destination within about a minute.
  • Every 15 minutes (background) — an Action Scheduler job retries anything still queued, processes abandoned carts, and mirrors state back where needed.

One‑time imports run when you press Start / Sync again and then continue in the background.

Smaily Connect töötab kahel kiirusel:

  • Peaaegu reaalajas — kui klient või tellimus muutub, käivitab WooCommerce hooki ja plugin paneb muudatuse kohe järjekorda. Reaalne muudatus (nt toote hind) jõuab sihtkohta tavaliselt umbes minutiga.
  • Iga 15 minuti järel (taust) — Action Scheduleri töö kordab kõike, mis veel järjekorras, töötleb hüljatud korve ja peegeldab seisu vajadusel tagasi.

Ühekordsed impordid käivituvad, kui vajutad Start / Sync again, ja jätkavad seejärel taustal.

Why do I see fewer orders than expected?Miks näen oodatust vähem tellimusi?

The Orders import brings in your full history — there is no time window. If a count looks low, it's because only real sales are counted: draft, pending, failed and trashed orders are excluded (they aren't completed sales), and a store with many orders often has far fewer unique customers because of repeat buyers.

Tellimuste import toob sisse su terve ajaloo — ajaakent pole. Kui number tundub madal, on põhjus, et loetakse ainult päris müügid: mustand‑, ootel, ebaõnnestunud ja prügikasti‑tellimused on välja jäetud (need pole lõpetatud müügid), ja paljude tellimustega poel on korduvostjate tõttu sageli palju vähem unikaalseid kliente.

Is my whole customer list imported?Kas terve kliendinimekiri imporditakse?

Yes. The customer import is not truncated. It sends every registered customer (and, if you enabled it, guest‑order emails). A count lower than you expect is usually repeat buyers collapsing into fewer unique customers — not a dropped record.

Jah. Klientide import ei ole kärbitud. See saadab iga registreeritud kliendi (ja kui lubasid, siis külalis‑tellimuste e‑postid). Oodatust madalam number on tavaliselt korduvostjad, kes koonduvad vähemateks unikaalseteks klientideks — mitte kaotatud kirje.

What does Disconnect do?Mida Disconnect teeb?

  • Disconnect Smaily — contact sync stops until you reconnect. Existing contacts and Smaily automations are untouched.
  • Disconnect Campaign Intelligence — recommendations and data ingest stop. Reconnecting needs a fresh one‑time setup link.
  • Disconnect Smaily — kontakti‑sünk peatub kuni taasühendamiseni. Olemasolevaid kontakte ja Smaily automaatikaid ei puudutata.
  • Disconnect Campaign Intelligence — soovitused ja andmevoog peatuvad. Taasühendamiseks on vaja värsket ühekordset seadistuslinki.

Does uninstalling delete my data?Kas eemaldamine kustutab andmed?

Uninstalling the plugin removes its settings and local queue data from your WordPress site. Your Smaily contacts and any Campaign Intelligence data persist — deliberately, so you can change platforms without losing your marketing history. Per‑customer erasure is always available (see below), and your stored Smaily credentials are removed on uninstall.

Plugina eemaldamine kustutab su WordPressi saidilt selle seaded ja kohaliku järjekorra andmed. Su Smaily kontaktid ja mis tahes Campaign Intelligence'i andmed jäävad alles — meelega, et saaksid platvormi vahetada ilma turundusajalugu kaotamata. Kliendipõhine kustutamine on alati saadaval (vt allpool) ja su salvestatud Smaily andmed eemaldatakse eemaldamisel.
